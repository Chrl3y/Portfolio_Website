# Monetization and Affiliate Plan

## Revenue stack

Do not rely on display advertising alone. Treat monetization as a layered system:

1. consulting/services leads;
2. affiliate referrals;
3. digital products/templates;
4. SaaS/tool referrals;
5. newsletter sponsorships;
6. direct sponsorships;
7. display advertising;
8. paid courses/workshops where relevant.

## Affiliate architecture

Create an affiliate registry instead of hard-coding links throughout articles.

Suggested record:

```yaml
merchant_id:
merchant_name:
program_name:
network:
category:
status: applied|approved|rejected|paused
countries_supported: []
payout_method:
payout_currency:
minimum_payout:
commission_type: recurring|one_time|cpa|cpl|hybrid
commission:
cookie_window:
tracking_base_url:
subid_supported:
approved_domains: []
disclosure_required: true
notes:
```

The content object should reference `merchant_id`, not directly embed an opaque affiliate URL where possible.

## Contextual matching

Affiliate insertion should be relevance-driven.

Example:

```text
Article: "How I deploy automation stacks on a VPS"
Relevant affiliates:
- hosting/VPS provider
- domain registrar
- backup/storage product
- monitoring tool

Not relevant:
- unrelated consumer electronics
- gambling
- speculative financial offers
```

Use rules + semantic matching to propose candidates. Require review until false-positive rates are known.

## Tracking

Every outbound affiliate click should record:
- article/content ID;
- merchant ID;
- placement ID;
- campaign ID;
- timestamp;
- channel/referrer;
- affiliate sub-ID where the program supports it.

Do not collect unnecessary personal data.

## Disclosure

Affiliate relationships must be disclosed clearly and close enough to the recommendation that a reader understands the relationship. Do not hide disclosure in a generic footer only.

Suggested reusable disclosure:

> Some links may be affiliate links. If you purchase through them, I may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Recommendations are selected for relevance, not solely because a commission is available.

Adapt language to local law, network rules, and destination-market requirements.

## Candidate categories for this portfolio

Strong fit:
- cloud/VPS hosting;
- domains/DNS/CDN;
- AI tools;
- developer tooling;
- automation platforms;
- analytics/BI tools;
- cybersecurity tools;
- productivity software;
- design/video tools;
- developer education/courses;
- hardware where the audience intent is genuinely purchase-oriented.

Avoid forcing affiliate content into personal portfolio/case-study pages where it damages trust.

## Programs to investigate

This is a research queue, not an assumption of approval or Uganda payout support:
- Vizard affiliate program;
- hosting providers with established affiliate programs;
- DigitalOcean referral/partner offerings;
- Canva affiliate/partner offerings where currently available;
- PartnerStack-listed SaaS programs;
- Impact/Rakuten/CJ/Awin programs relevant to software and developer audiences;
- direct affiliate programs from tools actually used in published tutorials.

Before activation, verify:
1. Uganda is accepted for publisher signup;
2. payout methods work locally;
3. tax/KYC requirements;
4. minimum payout;
5. whether paid-search bidding is restricted;
6. whether self-referrals are forbidden;
7. whether AI-generated promotional materials have specific restrictions.

## Display advertising ladder

Early:
- Google AdSense once the site has sufficient original content and policy pages.

Developer/technical audience later:
- Carbon Ads;
- EthicalAds;
- direct sponsorship inventory.

Higher traffic:
- evaluate premium networks based on then-current traffic/geography thresholds and economics.

Keep homepage, CV, contact, and flagship portfolio pages relatively clean. Put display monetization primarily in the Insights/Blog/Lab content area.

## Own-product monetization

The highest-margin opportunities may come from products generated by repeated work:
- architecture templates;
- SOPs;
- project changelog templates;
- automation workflow packs;
- analytics dashboards;
- code starter kits;
- consultation packages;
- implementation guides;
- paid workshop materials.

The performance agent should track product/lead conversion alongside ad/affiliate revenue so the system does not optimize for low-value clicks at the expense of high-value professional leads.
